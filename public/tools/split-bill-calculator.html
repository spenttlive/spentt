How to split a bill fairly

Splitting bills fairly among friends, family, or colleagues is something most people do weekly — but it often leads to confusion, awkward calculations, or someone paying more than they should. This calculator handles all three common scenarios.

  • Equal split: The simplest approach. Total bill divided by the number of people. Works best when everyone ordered roughly the same amount.
  • Percentage split: When one person ordered significantly more, or when you want to account for couples vs individuals. Flexible and fair.
  • Custom amount: When you want to track exactly what each person ordered. The most precise method for large groups or expensive meals.

Should you include tip in the split?

Yes — tip should always be split proportionally along with the bill. If you're splitting unequally (by percentage or custom amount), each person's tip contribution should match their share of the bill. This calculator handles this automatically for all three split modes.

Frequently asked questions

What's the easiest way to split a restaurant bill?
The easiest method is equal split — enter the total bill, add tip if applicable, add the number of people, and get the per-person amount instantly. For most casual dining with friends, this is the fairest and least awkward approach.
How do I split a bill when one person paid?
Select the person who paid in the "Who paid the bill?" field. The calculator will show who owes them and how much — a clear summary you can share directly on WhatsApp so everyone knows what they owe.
How should couples split bills in a group?
Couples typically split as two people (paying their combined share together) or as one combined unit (paying one share between them). The percentage split mode works well for this — assign the couple a combined percentage that reflects their order.
